Problem

Why do shoppers hesitate to buy clothing online?

Shoppers can't tell how clothes will fit, so they make poor purchase decisions and return more. Size guides and product photos don't offer the personalization needed to buy with confidence.

Inconsistent sizing

Sizing varies across brands, so choosing the right fit is guesswork.

Images vs. reality

Product photos don't always reflect real fit, fabric or quality.

No personal guidance

Without tailored recommendations, browsing is overwhelming and slow.

Return processes

Unclear or complicated return policies increase hesitation before buying.

Research

Key insights

What I did
  • Qualitative interviews and a survey (n=21) with online fashion shoppers
  • Analyzed return behavior linked to size and fit uncertainty
  • Reviewed Myntra, Zara and ASOS for personalization gaps
  • Studied mobile shopping behavior and decision drop‑off points
What I found
  • Fit uncertainty directly affects conversion and return rates
  • Most users shop on mobile, so visual clarity is critical
  • Users abandon purchases without body‑specific recommendations
  • Transparent returns reduce pre‑purchase anxiety
81%want AI virtual try‑on
71.4%say the return policy matters

Principles

Principles behind the solution

Make decisions faster

Surface fit scores, size recommendations and outfit suggestions at the moment of consideration.

Increase fit confidence

Show how a garment fits the user's own body on a personal AI avatar, not a generic model.

Reduce return anxiety

Clear size guidance, easy return initiation and visible delivery timelines.

Solution

Design solution

AI avatar setup (StyleMe)
Feature 01

AI avatar setup (StyleMe)

Users enter body measurements or pick a body type, skin tone and hairstyle to build a personal avatar. They can also create avatars for friends or family when shopping for gifts.

Virtual try‑on + fit insights
Feature 02

Virtual try‑on + fit insights

Garments are layered onto the avatar with a 3D preview, fit insights and size guidance. Users can mix and match, see color suggestions and browse trending outfits before adding to cart.

Checkout + returns clarity
Feature 03

Checkout + returns clarity

A streamlined cart with coupons, a clear shipping cost breakdown and a one‑tap return or exchange flow in the order view.

Impact

Impact and results

Improved purchase confidence

81% showed strong interest in AI try‑on. Usability testing showed more certainty in size selection and less hesitation at checkout.

Reduced decision friction

Streamlined onboarding and fit scoring shortened the path from browsing to purchase.

Conversion and return potential

42.9% said they would pay more for virtual try‑on, a signal of stronger purchase intent.

Reflection

Conclusion

Many e‑commerce platforms struggle with fit uncertainty, weak personalization and low purchase confidence. They rely on static visuals and generic sizing, which leads to hesitation and returns.

Fitable closes that gap by building AI avatars and virtual try‑on into the shopping flow. The project showed me how careful UX paired with AI can improve trust, satisfaction and engagement in online fashion.
