Problem

Why do people struggle to track job applications?

After applying, job seekers rarely know where they stand. Tracking is scattered and disorganized, which leads to missed follow‑ups, lower motivation and inconsistent effort.

No visibility

No updates after applying.

Scattered tracking

Applications live across apps, emails and notes.

Unclear hiring process

No sense of which stage an application is in.

Repetitive effort

The same details are entered again and again.

Research

Key insights

What I did
  • Interviews and a survey with job seekers
  • Identified drop‑offs caused by a lack of updates
  • Analyzed competitor platforms for tracking gaps
  • Studied how people follow up on applications
What I found
  • Missing updates after applying is the main reason people disengage
  • Most people track applications across several tools at once
  • Without clear stages, it's hard to know when to follow up
  • Re‑entering the same information adds avoidable effort

Reflection

Conclusion

Many job seekers struggle to track applications because of poor visibility and fragmented systems. Over time that leads to missed follow‑ups and lower engagement.

TalentConnect centralizes tracking and gives clear status updates, so people stay informed, organized and consistent, and follow through on more of their job search.
