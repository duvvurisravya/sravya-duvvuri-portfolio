About Me

UI/UX Designer
& Researcher.

I'm a UI/UX designer and researcher finishing my MA at San Diego State. I begin each project by understanding users through research, interviews and usability testing, then turn what people say into apps, websites and AI products that feel easy to use.

Every case study runs through the same five stages, scaled to the project: Empathize (interviews, surveys, competitor audits), Define (personas, empathy and journey maps), Ideate (user flows and information architecture), Design (wireframes to hi‑fi prototypes in Figma) and Test (Maze and moderated testing, then documented iterations).
