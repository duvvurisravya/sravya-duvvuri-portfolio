Sravya Duvvuri brand guide

One logo, one accent and three typefaces, used the same way on the portfolio, LinkedIn, Behance and the resume.

Wordmark

My name in Bricolage Grotesque. The dot of the final “i” is a speech bubble, because my work starts with what users say.

Mark

The bubble on its own: favicon, profile picture and small spaces.

Use the dark rounded square for profile pictures and app icons, and the plain bubble on light backgrounds.

Size and space

Below about 200px wide, use the small version: the bubble gets bigger and drops its dots.

Keep clear space around the logo equal to the height of the bubble. Never stretch, rotate, outline or recolor it.

Color

Peach is the only brand accent. It always marks the bubble and key highlights.

Voice

How the brand sounds.

DoLead with evidence: “Survey n=21”, “81% wanted try‑on”. Write plainly, in first person, in short sentences.
Don'tUse buzzwords or vague claims like “passionate pixel‑pusher”. Use emoji as decoration or more than one accent color.

Checklist

Use this everywhere your name appears.

  • Portfolio header and favicon use the wordmark and mark.
  • Resume header: full wordmark in Ink, about 28px tall.
  • LinkedIn and Behance: same banner and profile icon.
  • Email signature: small wordmark plus “UI/UX Designer · UX Researcher”.
  • Case study covers: project color for the project, peach for anything about me.
